Scope and limitations

Historical advertised inventory is not a representative sample of all US transactions. Source collection: CarGurus inventory, September 2020. No temporal generalization claim is made. VIN grouping reduces repeated-vehicle leakage; missing VINs use listing/hash fallback. Descriptions, dealer details and URLs are excluded from predictive features.
